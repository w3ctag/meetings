10. Define "what is finished"
11. Correct individuals where necessary
12. Chairs should consider the role to be a sevice role not a role of superiority
13. Continued support from co-chairs, increased sharing of load from non-chairs.  A culture of support. +2
14. Avoid stalling, move the work forward. Not pushing people to work more, but also more aggressively declining work items that are irrelevant
15. Chairs need to be careful answering "what the TAG thinks" only when/if the TAG actually has consensus on a topic.
16. Looking for new TAG candidates and associates
17. Recgonise when there isn't consensus and not identify it as a failing, but an opportunity just to move on. +5
18. Continue to evolve the work mode according to new ideas, new people, etc +1

23. Keep the group informal and friendly +1
