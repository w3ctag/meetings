# TAG Minutes - 18 Sept 2025 - Hong Kong F2F

## Retrospective

Present: Lola, Martin, Yves, Marcos, Jeffrey, Matthew, Sarven, Max

### How has this year gone?

* Strange year with people leaving, some not being here. Not that great to see people be absent. But we made progress. Better to deliver something good than something rushed.
* The associates were good. Not all participated, but we expected that.
* Felt ok, maybe a little slow in terms of output.
* We pumped out a lot of design reviews.
* Associates have been good. Needs more clarity. Worried about having too many chefs. Clarifying the role of associates relative to elected people.
* Did well on design reviews; significantly reduced backlog.
* We're doing a good job of focusing more on findings, especially about key issues. I have really good stuff to come. Should be proud.
* Started excited, had a dip, then picking up the pace recently.
* Increased focus on findings was great.
* Want more clarity on associates; better way to distinguish from core members.
* Surprised by becoming a chair so early in TAG tenure. That transition has been as smooth as it could be.
* Group has done a lot of good. And there's a lot of bad in the world. Struggling with how we fit into all of that, and how we use our limited power. We're not doing as much as we could, but we have limitations.
* Not everyone has spent as much time as we should have on doing TAG work. Lots of variation within the group, and not always for good reasons.
* Chairing has been great. And the team as a whole.
* Concerned about outside-of-breakout time that we dedicate. In addition to setting an expectation for incoming TAG people, wonder if there are expectations for Member Organizations. You're nominated by a member organization; not enough to communicate to electees, but also to organizations.
* We should give more direct feedback.
* People on the TAG have different abilities and interests. We've done some work to find ways for people to contribute to what they're interested in, but not enough. We need to be more intentional about this in future, asking people what they want to do, and looking for other ideas, to find ways for people to contribute effectively to things they care about.
* Being an Invited Expert on TAG is difficult. Celebrate those at member companies who make the process easier, and the W3C for the Invited Expert fund. There's a way up (?) taking paid work vs TAG work. IEs bring value to TAG.
* Historical TAGs have often used TAGness to take another bite of the apple (after losing an argument in a WG). I think this TAG is doing better at this.

## What would you like to change?

* In Paris, we said we'd stop using cryptpad, and we didn't. Maybe someone needs to find an alternative.
* Sometimes people are assuming that the chairs know best - I would appreciate more input into how things run.
* The women on the TAG are being accidentally patronized.
